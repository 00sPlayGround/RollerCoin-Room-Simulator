Rollercoin Room simulator gives you, the user, better flexibility and ability to place your miners without the fear of accidentally going to another league.
All you have to do is import your room, import your inventory and that's it. all data is in your pc offline and you can play with your room as much as you would like.

This is version 1 and in this release you can expect:

1. Importing your room via your ID
2. Manually importing your inventory.
3. Adding new or missing miners
4. Adding new or missing racks
5. Adding Sets
6. placing racks in any of the rooms
7. placing miners on any racks that are placed in the room
8. seeing your total power from all placed racks and miners
9. seeing breakdown of your total power.
10. seeing in what league you will end up in.
11. Seeing what miners, from your personal inventory, can be leveled up
12. Seeing what miners, from your personal inventory, are sellable with direct link to sellable miners
13. Auto Set function (Needs work)
14. Changing Room background
15. Placing and removing decorations (might be buggy atm and can't be placed on top of rack placeholders)
16. Pet Hamster (currently only 1)

IMPORTANT INFORMATION:
Some miners/racks can and most likely will be missing, do not worry you can add them manually. After adding, restart the application and you will have those missing miners/racks in the app.

By default new miners/racks will be in "store" section and not in your personal inventory.
After that you will be able to add the missing miners to your inventory. You will have to either import the room again, or add missing miners manually.
