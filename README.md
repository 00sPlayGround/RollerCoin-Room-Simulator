Rollercoin Room simulator gives you, the user, better flexibility and ability to place your miners without the fear of accidentally going to another league.
All you have to do is import your room, import your inventory and that's it. All data is in your pc, offline and you can play with your room as much as you would like, whenever you will like.


IMPORTANT INFORMATION:
Some miners/racks can and most likely will be missing, do not worry you can add them manually. After adding, restart the application and you will have those missing miners/racks in the app.

By default new miners/racks will be in "store" section and not in your personal inventory.
After that you will be able to add the missing miners to your room or inventory manually or re import your room/inventory again.

Disclaimer:

You are highly advised to check, double check and triple check everything, be it state of your room after importing, inventory importing or math done by the application.
Currently no issues/bugs have been observed when importing player room or player inventory, except occasional missing miners/racks, that should be expected as database might not be up to date.

Please be mindful that YOU are responsible for your actions when using the application, so again check and double check everything.

In case of encountering any bugs, please feel free to notify me in discord, explain the issue. If I am able to have it fixed, great and if not, tough luck. 

Any suggestions for improving the app is always welcome.
